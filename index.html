Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Plotline authorized to provide investment advice?

No. Plotline is an educational hackathon simulation designed to showcase programmatic financial data extraction and constrained LLM portfolio analysis. It is not regulated by the FCA or any financial authority.

How does the engine prevent AI hallucinations?

All numerical metrics (returns, volatility, drawdown) are computed explicitly in Python code. The LLM is provided a strictly schema-validated JSON format and is forbidden from generating ungrounded statistics. A secondary verification step checks every cited figure against the calculated table.

How is the private architecture venture scored?

The private raise sits in an unlisted venture slot outside the public equity layers. It is evaluated qualitatively as Go, Partial, or No-go based on client risk criteria. If rated "No-go", its allocation drops to 0% and capital is retained in cash or re-allocated to public layers.

How are foreign exchange fluctuations handled?

While the investment pot is denominated in USD ($100,000), UK-listed assets trade in GBP (£). Conversions use daily spot exchange rates. Currency fluctuations contribute to total portfolio volatility.
